Frequency domain

A sinusoidal signal can be uniquely represented by uniformly spaced samples only when its frequency is below half the sampling frequency. Frequencies above the Nyquist frequency are reflected or folded into the observable frequency range and therefore appear as aliases.

Cadence sets the maximum recoverable frequency. Decreasing Δt raises fs and the Nyquist frequency.

Duration sets the Fourier frequency resolution. Increasing T makes the frequency bins finer, approximately Δf = 1/T.

Try 1, 3, and 5 Hz with a cadence of 0.25 s: all three produce a peak near 1 Hz. A finite observation can spread power across nearby bins when it contains a non-integer number of cycles.
